## Notes

- hreflang is paired only where a genuine EN equivalent exists (gated by `hasEnApp`/`hasEnGuide` in code); untranslated apps get no `en-US` alternate. Verified by the check:seo language-parity check added in Phase 3.
- Tools pair via `hasEnTool` (explicit idSlug → enPath mapping) because tool slugs differ between editions. The TikTok tool is localized via a `lang` prop on the shared component (SEO-18), so the English hub's embedded tool and the dedicated English page are both English; the Instagram downloader has its own English component (SEO-17).
- EN pages are adaptations, not word-for-word translations: examples and search intent are rewritten for English readers; facts, developer names, official links and check dates are identical in both languages (stated on the EN homepage).
