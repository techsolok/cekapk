## 6. Terms and DMCA improvements
- **Terms (both editions, 13 sections):** acceptance, permitted use, prohibited use, user responsibility for downloaded content, copyright/IP, APK information and official download sources, downloader service limitations, service availability and changes, security and abuse prevention, third-party links, reporting and complaint handling, limitation of liability (worded "to the extent permitted by applicable law" — no unreasonable or misleading clauses), contact and changes to the terms. Built on the original 3 paragraphs; nothing in the original was contradicted.
- **DMCA (both editions, 8 sections — corrected 8 Nov 2026):** the pages are framed as CekAPK's **voluntary** copyright-complaint and content-removal process, with an explicit statement that this is **not** a representation that CekAPK has established every element of the formal US DMCA statutory process, and that the page is not legal advice. Sections: who may submit (owner or authorized agent); the information that helps review (identification, work identification, exact URL, good-faith statement) — presented as a practical guide, **not** a complete statutory notice checklist; the review process (2-business-day **internal target**, not a legal deadline; hosted-vs-linked distinction; outcome explained; recorded on Transparency); **material hosted or linked by third parties** (action may require the external host or copyright owner; CekAPK can review its own pages and links, including removing a link, but cannot promise removal from third-party servers); **responding to a removal decision or reporting a mistaken removal** — responses are reviewed individually, **no automatic restoration**, and the statutory 10–14-business-day procedure **does not apply**; false-notice warning. No registered DMCA agent, address, phone number, legal status, or safe-harbor eligibility is claimed. No absolute "all files are legally verified" claim — the intro says files have *passed our verification pipeline* (qualified, accurate). The DMCA email is a mailto link in both editions. **If CekAPK later wants a formal statutory DMCA process, that needs appropriate legal review and proper designated-agent arrangements first — flagged, not claimed.**
