DMCA & Content Removal

Last updated: 8 November 2026 - Applies to CekAPK.

CekAPK respects copyright. Our policy is not to host copyrighted works without permission; our app pages are informational, and download buttons point to official sources or to files that have passed our verification pipeline.

Our Voluntary Reporting Process

This page describes CekAPK's voluntary reporting and review procedure for copyright complaints. It is not a representation that CekAPK has established every element of the formal US DMCA statutory process, and this page is not legal advice. If you need a formal statutory process, consult a legal adviser.

Who May Submit a Complaint

A copyright complaint may be submitted by the copyright owner, or by an agent authorized to act on the owner's behalf. If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, state your relationship to the owner and your authority to act for them.

Information That Helps Us Review Your Report

Send your report to dmca@cekapk.com and include the following information so we can review it: (1) your identification and contact details, (2) identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed — enough detail for us to find it, (3) the exact URL on our site where the allegedly infringing material appears, and (4) a good-faith statement that you believe the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law. This list is our practical guide for reviewing reports — not a complete checklist of statutory DMCA notice requirements. A report that does not include the exact URL or a clear identification of the work is hard for us to process.

How We Review Reports

Our internal target is to review within 2 business days — this is our internal target, not a legal deadline. We check the reported URL, the identified work, and whether the material is hosted by us or only linked to an official source. When a report is valid, we aim to take appropriate action within that internal target; cases that require additional investigation may take longer. We will inform the reporter of the outcome; when a report is not valid, or when the material is only an informational page linking to an official source, we explain why. The number of copyright reports we receive is shown as an aggregate count on our Transparency page; no complainant personal information is published there.

Material Hosted or Linked by Third Parties

When the material complained about sits on a third-party server, or when our page only links to it, action against that material may require the external host or the copyright owner. We can review our own pages and links — including removing a problematic link — but we cannot promise to remove material from third-party servers.

Responding to a Removal Decision or Reporting a Mistaken Removal

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, or you are entitled to the material, send a response to dmca@cekapk.com with: your identification and contact details, identification of the material and where it appeared on our site, and an explanation of why the removal was a mistake or why you are entitled to use the material. We review every response individually; there is no automatic restoration. The statutory 10–14-business-day procedure does not apply here — we review responses against the same 2-business-day internal target, and each decision is made case by case.

False or Careless Notices

A false or careless notice can harm others; please make sure your claim is correct before sending it. We do not accept notices that do not identify a specific work or a specific URL on our site.
