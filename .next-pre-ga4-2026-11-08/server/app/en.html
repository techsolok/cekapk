Know an app before you install it.

CekAPK is an Indonesian site built on one rule: official sources first. We write editorial guides to popular Android apps — what they do, what permissions they ask for, and how to stay safe — host only open-source files that pass a public verification pipeline (hash, developer signature, multi-engine scan, permission audit), and offer free tools that never ask for your login. This English edition covers our most-read pages; the full catalog of 50 apps lives in the Indonesian edition.
